Settings page

Set it up from your phone

Once Ai-DeskBuddy is on Wi-Fi it shows its address on screen for 10 seconds. Open it in any browser on the same network. You can also ask “open the settings page”.

Tab 1

Weather

Display

Install

Flash it from this page

Plug Ai-DeskBuddy into your computer with a USB-C data cable and install the firmware straight from your browser. Nothing to download.

1. Pick your screen

2. Connect and install

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or flash the .bin file at address 0x0 with esptool. Flashing needs a secure (https) page. Open this site from its GitHub Pages address.

Works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS and Linux. If no port appears, hold BOOT on the S3-Zero while plugging in USB, then try again.

Prefer manual? Download 1.3" firmware or 0.96" firmware and flash it at 0x0.

  1. FlashPick your screen type, click Connect & install and choose the USB serial port.
  2. Join its Wi-FiOn first boot Ai-DeskBuddy creates a Wi-Fi hotspot and shows its name on screen. Join it from your phone and enter your home Wi-Fi.
  3. Activate the AIIt then shows a 6-digit code. Add the device with that code in the xiaozhi.me console to pick the voice and AI model.
  4. Add your weather keyOpen the settings page shown on screen and enter your city and a free OpenWeather API key. The public firmware ships without one.
  5. Say “Hi ESP”That's it. Hold the touch pad 5 seconds to try the other modes.
